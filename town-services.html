Town Government

  • Town Hall

    Cromwell Town Hall

    The main point of contact for town business.

    Address: 41 West Street, Cromwell, CT 06416

    Website: www.cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Cromwell is governed under a Mayor–Council system. (Source: cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab))

    Open in Maps ↗(opens in a new tab)

The validated research files record the Town Hall address but not its telephone number or office hours. Those are deliberately omitted here rather than guessed — check cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab) for current hours and contact numbers.

Public Works

  • Department

    Cromwell Public Works Department

    Handles public grounds, roads and town infrastructure.

    Address: 41 West Street, Cromwell, CT 06416

    Website: Public Works ↗(opens in a new tab)

    For parks, roads and public grounds questions, start here. (Source: Cromwell Public Works ↗(opens in a new tab))

Schools

  • District

    Cromwell Public Schools

    Cromwell Middle School and Cromwell High School serve the town's students.

    Website: cromwell.k12.ct.us ↗(opens in a new tab)

    For enrolment, calendars, term dates and district announcements, use the district site. (Source: Cromwell Schools ↗(opens in a new tab))
