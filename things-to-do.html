Things to Do in Cromwell, Connecticut

Cromwell is a river town, and that shapes almost everything worth doing here.

The Connecticut River runs along the town, the town covers 13.5 square miles of Middlesex County, and the community calendar follows the seasons. (Source: Cromwell Town Profile ↗(opens in a new tab))

Here's how to plan your time — and where to look for the specifics.

Start with the River

Cromwell's defining feature is its Connecticut River frontage. Sitting on the river between Hartford to the north and New Haven to the south means waterfront views, river valley scenery, and access to the wider Connecticut River corridor that runs through Middlesex County.

If you're planning a visit, build the day around the water and work outward.

Landmarks & Local Character

Cromwell's history runs back to 1651, when the first settlers arrived in what was then the Upper Houses section of Middletown — two centuries before the town incorporated in 1851. A town with that much history has landmarks worth documenting. (Source: Cromwell Town Profile ↗(opens in a new tab))

Seasonal Events & Community Life

Cromwell's community calendar is run by the town and its schools and organisations. The Cromwell Library (cromwellct.gov/library ↗(opens in a new tab)) and Cromwell Public Schools (cromwell.k12.ct.us ↗(opens in a new tab)) both publish community programming through the year, and the town website is the source for official events.

Where to check:

  • Cromwell town website ↗(opens in a new tab) — official events and notices
  • Cromwell Library ↗(opens in a new tab) — library programming
  • Cromwell Public Schools ↗(opens in a new tab) — school and district calendar

Nearby

Cromwell's location makes it a good base. It's roughly 14 miles south of Hartford and 28 miles north of New Haven, so both cities' museums, dining and entertainment are within easy reach — and Middlesex County's river valley towns are next door. (Source: cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab))
