Contact Cromwell

Whether you need a town department, a service, or something about this site, here's where to start.

Cromwell Town Hall

  • Town Hall

    Cromwell Town Hall

    41 West Street, Cromwell, CT 06416

    Telephone: (860) 632-3410

    Website: www.cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Town Hall is the main contact point for town business in Cromwell. The town operates under a Town Council / Town Manager form of government — an elected Town Council sets policy and a Town Manager runs day-to-day administration. Cromwell has no mayor.

    (Source: Form of Government ↗(opens in a new tab))

About This Site

This is a community information site for Cromwell, Connecticut. It's a static guide — no live news service and no ticketing.

  • Found an error? Tell us — accuracy matters more than completeness here, and every fact on this site is traceable to a public source.
    Contact route pending
  • Want your business listed? See Local Businesses → — listings come from public directories at no charge.
  • Have a community notice? See News & Events →.
  • Need something official? Always use cromwellct.gov ↗(opens in a new tab) — our links point there for exactly that reason.

There is no contact form on this site: it is built as static pages, so there is no endpoint to receive a message, and no email address for this site has been verified. The route for corrections is still being set up — until it is, please use the official town channels above.
