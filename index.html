Welcome to Cromwell, Connecticut

A river town in Middlesex County with 14,225 neighbours, deep roots, and an easy pace.

Cromwell sits on the Connecticut River halfway between Hartford and New Haven — 14 miles south of the capital, 28 miles north of New Haven. Settled in 1651 and incorporated as its own town in 1851, it has spent three centuries being the kind of place people drive through once and then decide to stay. (Source: Cromwell Town Profile ↗(opens in a new tab))

This guide gathers what residents and visitors ask for most: what the town is, what's here, who to call, and what's happening. Start anywhere below.

New to Town?

Three things worth doing in your first week:

  1. Find Town Hall.

    41 West Street, Cromwell, CT 06416 — the address for most official business, and home to several departments. (Directions & info ↗(opens in a new tab))

  2. Get your water sorted.

    Connecticut Water serves the town — useful to know before you unpack. (connecticutwater.com ↗(opens in a new tab))

  3. Get a library card.

    The Cromwell Library is a good shortcut to local knowledge and community noticeboards. (Library ↗(opens in a new tab))
